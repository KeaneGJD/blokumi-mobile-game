Blokumi Privacy Policy

Effective 28 August 2026

Blokumi is developed by Nah Then Games. This policy explains how the Android game handles information.

Information we collect

Blokumi does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. The game does not include advertising, analytics, user accounts or cloud tracking.

Information stored on your device

Scores, achievements, settings, Daily progress and other game progress are stored locally on your device. Nah Then Games cannot access this information. It can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

Sharing results

If you choose to share a result, Blokumi creates a result card and opens your device's sharing controls. Nothing is shared until you choose a destination. The selected third-party app handles the shared content under its own privacy policy.

External services

Blokumi includes optional links to Google Play and the Nah Then Games community on Discord. A confirmation is shown before leaving Blokumi for Discord. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Blokumi does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Blokumi's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is made.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to nahthengames@gmail.com.
