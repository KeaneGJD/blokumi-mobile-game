How to play

  1. Press and drag one of the three blocks onto the board.
  2. The highlighted cells show exactly where it will be placed.
  3. Fill a complete row or column to clear it and score.
  4. Place all three blocks to receive a fresh set.
  5. Before placing from a set, tap the circular randomise button to refresh all three blocks. You have two uses per run.
  6. Orange Blast Blocks detonate when their row or column clears, destroying occupied tiles within two spaces.
  7. Purple Cross Blocks clear occupied tiles along their opposite axis when their row or column clears.
  8. In Endless and Rush, activate the meter at Tier I or hold it for stronger Tier II and Tier III powers.
  9. The run ends when none of your remaining blocks fit.

Clear lines on consecutive moves to grow your combo and score faster.

Block deliveries become more challenging as your run progresses, with larger shapes joining the pool later.

Daily Shard Hunt is objective-only: clear 10 glowing Shards from the pre-built board and supplied blocks before 12 sets run out. Daily has no score, randomiser or Power Meter.

Blokumi Rush begins with 60 seconds and a pre-built board. Clear the full board to load another pattern. Marked tiles award +5 or +10 seconds when removed.

Endless scoring: each placed tile earns 5 points. Clears add tile and line bonuses, then apply combo and active Power Mode multipliers.
